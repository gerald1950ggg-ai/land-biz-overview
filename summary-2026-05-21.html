3. County Selection — Reviewed Together

Reviewed the county audit at the meeting. Key findings acknowledged:

  • Osceola County, FL — failed the donut method (inside the Orlando MSA, only 17-22 miles from downtown). Both agreed to replace it.
  • Kaufman County, TX — non-disclosure state flag noted. Extra comp work required. Texas growth still compelling.
  • Tennessee (White County) — discussed positively. Both like the Nashville/Knoxville dual-metro positioning.
  • North Carolina counties — Marcus's research-driven picks; Chris familiar with NC from past work.
Decision: Start with THREE counties.
  1. One in Colorado — local, familiar market; Marcus researching which county passes the criteria (terrain/desert can be challenging)
  2. One in Tennessee — both positive on TN; Nashville orbit most likely
  3. One hot market from the audit — likely Franklin County NC or Marion County FL (to be confirmed)

Additional context: Both want to cross-reference selected counties against current land investor activity — what are people actually saying now on forums and social media, not just static data. Gerald to build this as a research tool.

4. Property Types — Two Types Agreed

Decision: Target two property types.
  • Type 1: 2-3 acre buildable lots in growth corridors — desirable, can build a house, sell to people wanting land near a city
  • Type 2: 5-30 acre recreational parcels — hunting, camping, getaway; market to city dwellers wanting their own piece of land

Infill lots considered too hard to source for a first campaign. Property type will determine county selection and list filters. Goal: narrow to one type per county for the first mailing.

5. Mailing Lists — How We Get Them

  • Platform: ListSource — Chris has used it; can filter by county, price point, owner age, out-of-state, years owned, and more
  • Ideal owner profile: out-of-state owner + long hold time (10+ years) + right acreage range + older owner
  • Probate / inherited land = highest quality leads; owners often have no emotional attachment
  • First campaign size: ~1,000 mailers (500 not enough for meaningful data)
  • Timeline: Within one month
  • Gerald action item: Research how LPG list system works vs. pulling directly from ListSource or similar

6. Phone / Response System

  • LandCalls.com mentioned as an answering service option (pre-screens calls before routing to Chris)
  • Both cautious about foreign-accent virtual assistants — rural land sellers respond poorly
  • For first campaign: Google Voice or similar; Marcus's number routes calls to Chris
  • Future: AI phone agent that captures seller info automatically before routing

7. Gerald — New Action Items

  • Live sold-to-active ratio tool: Build a sub-agent that navigates Zillow/LandWatch and pulls current sold-to-active ratios for any county on demand
  • Current investor sentiment: Access land investing forums, Reddit, BiggerPockets, X (via Grokboy) to cross-reference counties against what active investors are saying right now
  • List source research: How does the LPG system pull lists? Can Gerald replicate that without the $200/month subscription?
  • Landing page: Build atlasmorgan.com/land

8. Long-Term Vision

Both got excited about the bigger picture:

  • Chris's goal: $10K/month each reliably, ~20 hours/week — not a 60-hour grind
  • Seller financing as a passive income layer: once deals flow, offer buyers monthly payment terms
  • RV parks near national parks — floated as a future play; both found it compelling ("those things get booked out")
  • Development / rezoning as a long-term value-add angle on acquired land
  • Chris's model: "10% of a million vs. 100% of $10K — I'll take the 10% every time"
