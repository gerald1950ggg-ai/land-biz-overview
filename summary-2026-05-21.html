7. Land Acquisition Strategy

  • Blind offer mailers are the primary acquisition method: send unsolicited written offers to landowners at approximately 20 cents on the dollar.
  • Clarification: a blind offer is a written offer letter, not an actual check. The check comes at closing.
  • Legal escape clauses are essential (e.g., "subject to partner approval") so offers can be rescinded if needed.
  • Escrow payments are minimal ($1 to $100) to establish contract consideration.
  • Double-close strategy: contract the property, find a buyer, close both transactions simultaneously without needing to hold capital.
  • Even in competitive markets, timing matters: the goal is to reach a seller on the day they are ready to sell.
