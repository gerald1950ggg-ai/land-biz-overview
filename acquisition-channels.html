🎯 Multi-Channel Acquisition Strategy

Going beyond direct mail — every channel that reaches motivated land sellers
🤝 Chris Spiewak 🤖 Gerald AI ⚡ Coose 📅 May 9, 2026

The Premise

Direct mail is the commodity play. Every land flipper does it. It works — but when everyone uses the same channel with the same templates, the edge erodes. Our AI system already makes our mail smarter (personalized per parcel, targeted by intelligence). But the bigger opportunity is owning channels that nobody else in land flipping is running.

💡 A solo investor running Investment Dominator picks one channel. We run all of them simultaneously, track cost-per-deal by channel, and shift budget to what's working. That's not operationally possible without AI management.

Probate & Inheritance
Best Lead Quality
CostLow (public records)
MotivationVery high
DemoHeirs, estate executors
PhasePhase 2
Probate court records are public. When someone dies and land passes to heirs, those heirs often want to liquidate quickly — they didn't choose to own land, they inherited a tax liability. These are the most motivated sellers in the market and almost no land flippers are systematically finding them.
  • Monitor county probate filings → identify newly inherited land
  • AI triggers personalized outreach to executor or heir automatically
  • Lead quality: exceptional — time pressure + no emotional attachment to the land
  • Often priced to move fast (heirs want cash, not property management)
  • Works alongside standard mail — separate list, separate campaign
🤖 Build Target Phase 2: AI monitors probate filings → auto-triggers outreach. Feeds into List Engine as dedicated segment.

Tax Delinquent Lists
Supplemental
CostFree (public records)
MotivationHigh (financial pressure)
DemoOwners 2+ yrs delinquent
PhasePhase 2
Counties publish lists of owners who haven't paid property taxes in 2+ years. These owners are often checked out on their land, may not know what to do with it, and are facing eventual tax sale. They can be highly receptive to an offer — it solves a problem they've been ignoring.
  • Free data from county assessor / tax collector offices
  • Dedicated campaign separate from main absentee owner list
  • Messaging angle: "We can help you avoid tax sale and get cash now"
  • Layer with standard mail or SMS outreach
🤖 Phase 2: AI pulls and processes county tax delinquent lists. Feeds as dedicated segment into Parcel Personalization Engine.

✅ The System Advantage

A solo land flipper with Investment Dominator picks one channel, runs it manually, and hopes for the best. They have no way to know if their money is going to the right counties, the right owners, or the right message.

We run every channel simultaneously. Every inbound lead — whether from a mailer, a Facebook ad, a Google search, or a probate filing — flows into the same Response Scoring Engine. Chris sees a single ranked daily feed. The system learns which channels produce the best deals and shifts budget automatically. No other land flipper is operating this way.
