Steps 1–12 · Three Sides of the Business
Side 1 — Acquisition
01
County Selection
Pick 2–3 counties using demand analysis. Donut method: 30–100mi from metro. Target markets $5K–$300K. Sold/listed ratio, path of growth indicators.
🧠
County Intelligence Engine: Automated county scoring across 12+ signals — sold/listed ratio, median price, DOM, flood risk, zoning. Daily monitoring + alerts when a county heats up or cools off.
↓
02
List Building
Pull vacant land owner data — absentee owners, 1–100 acres, $5K–$300K assessed value. Filter out government entities, corporations, and banks.
🤖
List Engine: PropStream / ATTOM API integration. Auto-applies all filters. Deduplication, skip-trace enrichment, owner equity scoring. Export ready for mailing house.
↓
03
Direct Mail Campaign
Neutral letters sent to every owner. Mailing house handles print, stamp, and send. Low cost per mailer ($0.50–$0.85). Volume is the game.
✉️
Parcel Personalization Engine: Unique AI-written letter per owner — references parcel size, county, nearby sales. Sent via Lob.com API. No templates, no robots. Response rates 2–5x standard.
↓
04
Inbound Response Handling
Sellers call in. Transcribe conversation. Qualify the lead. Route to offer or reject. Time-sensitive — speed wins deals.
📊
Response Scoring Engine (ML): Auto-transcribe calls. Score seller motivation 0–100. Flag hot leads (score >75) for immediate callback. Auto-log parcel data. Chris handles final conversation.
↓
05
Offer Research
Comp the property — LandWatch, Zillow, Realtor.com. 20–30% accuracy is sufficient for offers. Speed matters more than perfection.
🗺️
AI Comp Engine: Auto-pull comps from LandWatch + Zillow + county records. Generate offer range: low / mid / high. Flagged for review in <60 seconds. No manual Zillow scrolling.
↓
06
Make Offers
Send written offers at 5–35% of market value. Most sellers say no. That's fine — you need 1–3 yeses per campaign. Volume is everything.
📝
Offer Templates: AI-drafted offer letters. Chris reviews and approves. Batch send capability. Auto-track sent/accepted/declined status per parcel.
↓
07
Process Acceptances
Contracts, earnest money, title company, escrow. Title does the heavy lifting. 30–45 days to close. Clean, documented, repeatable.
DD
Due Diligence
Gate between buying and selling. Verify everything before committing capital. Catch problems now — not at closing or after you own it.
📍 Market Value
Comp the property — LandWatch, Zillow, Realtor.com. Confirm offer was priced at 20–40% of true value.
🗺️ GIS / Plat Maps
Verify legal boundaries, parcel shape, lot dimensions. Flag anything irregular.
🚗 Access
Does the parcel have road access? Landlocked = nearly unsellable. Check deeded easements.
📋 Zoning
Confirm allowable uses. Flag restrictions, wetlands, flood zones, conservation easements.
💸 Liens & Back Taxes
Check for outstanding tax balances, HOA liens, judgments. Must clear before deed transfer.
🔌 Utilities
Power, water, septic availability. Significantly affects buyer pool and listing price.
🤖
AI DD Engine: Auto-pulls GIS data, county assessor records, flood maps, and zoning. Generates a DD summary card per parcel. Flags deal-killers before Coose or Chris spend time on a bad deal. Source: due-diligence-checklist.md (LPG).

Note Management / Sale — Continue collecting or sell note to investor at discount for lump sum.

Drip Engine: Auto-follow-up with finance buyers. Payment reminders. Note portfolio tracker.
