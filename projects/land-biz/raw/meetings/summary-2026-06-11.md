### 2. Call System Design
- Consensus reached on the structure: **live agent first, then route to partners**.
  - Inbound call → live agent (Pat Live or similar) picks up immediately
  - Agent filters "take me off your list" calls — partners never hear those
  - Genuine sellers are transferred live to both partners (simultaneous ring)
  - If neither partner answers, agent takes a name/number and texts both — one calls back immediately
- **No press-1 filters**: the 10–20% of callers who won't push buttons are the most likely real leads. A human answer is the differentiator.
- **Single land-specific number** — trackable to land only, separate from any other Atlas Morgan activity.
- Disposition calls may need a different number eventually, but not a priority now.

### 6. Comping Strategy — Triangulation Engine
- Gerald to build a repeatable **triangulation comp engine** for land:
  1. PropStream estimate
  2. CAD (County Appraisal District) appraised value
  3. HiLo estimate (or similar)
- Produces a range → offer at ~20–25% of that range (Jack Bosch / land flip methodology).
- Goal: be able to comp a parcel quickly on a call or ahead of a blind offer campaign.
- Current Gerald data sources: LandWatch (active listings) + RealQuest. Partners want to verify these numbers are solid — not just estimated.
- **Gerald task**: Build a repeatable, documented county search skill — a repeatable protocol with guardrails so outputs are actually research-backed, not AI-guessed.

### 7. Mailer Launch Strategy — Start Small
- Both partners agreed: **don't blast full mailers until systems are tested**.
- First run: ~100 mailers per county to verify:
  - Does the phone ring?
  - Does Pat Live route correctly?
  - Does the simultaneous ring work?
  - What's the breakdown of legit calls vs. take-me-off-the-list calls?
  - Any list quality issues?
- Then scale to ~1,000 mailers per county per round.
- Blind offer approach (20% of comped value) is the preferred opening strategy — efficient and filters for motivated sellers.

### 8. Personalized / Targeted Mailers
- Discussion about customizing mailer messages to specific owner profiles (not just a name swap):
  - Inherited property → softer, empathetic message
  - Divorce situation → quick resolution angle
  - Tax delinquent → stress relief angle
- Inspired by Gary Vaynerchuk hyper-local targeting logic: the more specific the message, the higher the open/response rate.
- **Not necessarily for the first blast** — but something to build toward as systems mature.
- Skip-tracing data and owner-type tagging can inform message variants.
