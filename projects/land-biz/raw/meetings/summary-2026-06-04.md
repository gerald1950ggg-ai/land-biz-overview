- **Pat Live configuration option**: Can be set up to just answer, say "Thanks for calling Atlas Morgan Land, one of the partners will call you right back," and take a name/number — no need to run through qualifying questions at scale if volume is low.
- **For low lead volume** (e.g., 3 leads/month): probably not worth the overhead. Partners should just pick up. But even one missed deal is costly if it was the only deal that month.
- **Ideal system**: Inbound number rings both partners first; if unanswered in ≤4 rings, routes to a live answering service (Pat Live or similar). Agent takes brief note, texts both partners, one calls back immediately.
- **Key insight**: People call when they're ready to talk *in that moment*. 9 out of 10 won't leave a voicemail. Being the person who picks up — or quickly returns — is the differentiator.

### 5. Land Strategy & Philosophy
- Land flipping is essentially the same game as other motivated-seller real estate — just a different property type.
- **Land is simpler** in many ways (no inspection issues, no tenants, no permits for basic transactions), but the marketing and comping require different skills.
- Two tracks discussed:
  1. **Quick-turn smaller lots** (1–4 acres infill): More available inventory, faster churn, lower margin per deal but more repeatable.
  2. **Larger entitlement plays** (10–50+ acres): Bigger money potential, longer hold, harder to find exit channel — but once cracked, much easier.
- **Expect blind alleys**: may find certain land types are hard to sell. Treat as learning, not failure.
