## Brand Name — STILL OPEN (Marcus owns)

**Current state:** Marcus to finalize in next couple of days, Chris has veto right.

**Marcus's leaning:** 60% Acreline Land / 40% Atlas Morgan Land

### Names Discussed
- **Acreline Land** — Marcus's leading candidate. Pros: "acre" implies acreage, "line" implies boundary line; 4 syllables; clean digital footprint (no overlap). Con: slight L-slur tongue twister ("Acreline Land")
- **Atlas Morgan Land** — Marcus's alternate. Pros: ties to existing 13-yr Atlas Morgan brand (atlas-morgan.com owned), sister-company credibility. Con: heavy digital noise around "Atlas Land" generally
- **Marlowe Green** — Gerald's pitch from earlier session. Marcus liked it: *"Marlowe Green Land Company — fuck yes."* Domain available (marlowegreen.com), no US LLC collision, soft UK conflict only
- **Slate variants** — Slate Grid, Slate Late, "anything Slate" considered. Both liked the word "slate" — single syllable, spellable, foundation feel
- **Other explored:** Verde, Apex, Tillage, Strata, Acreridge (taken in Australia), Grid Line Land, Acreline, dirt-themed, Boundary, Plumbob, Plumline

### Brand Criteria (agreed)
- Established / trustworthy / rooted feel
- 4 syllables or less
- Spellable, speakable, searchable
- Clean digital footprint (huge differentiator — land brand space is over-saturated)
- Memorable, not generic
