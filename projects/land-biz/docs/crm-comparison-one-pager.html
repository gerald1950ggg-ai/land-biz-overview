Why Airtable Wins (Phase 1)

  • No address-required friction — raw land runs on APNs; every dedicated REI platform was built for houses
  • Gerald integrates cleanly — full REST API, no Zapier middleware tax
  • PropStream already covers data — skip tracing + lead exports are redundant on dedicated platforms
  • Effectively $0 to start — free tier handles 1,000+ records per base
  • Auditability — every change timestamped, full revision history, no black boxes
  • Paul Brewer's land template exists — pre-built base for property tracking, mailing, ad posting

What to Skip & Why

  • FreedomSoft — built for house wholesaling; BBB D- rating; add-ons ($997 MailNow, $297 Dig Tool) bloat cost; aggressive upselling reported
  • REsimpli Pro — confirmed address-required bug for raw land (BiggerPockets 2023 thread); $299/mo is most expensive; only plan with 2-user support
  • Investment Dominator (LPG default) — already dismissed by Chris: overpriced, workflow-locked, frequent support issues

Phase 2 Trigger (Reassess When)

  • 5–10 closed deals completed
  • Workflow structure becomes the bottleneck
  • Then upgrade to: REIPro Standard $69/mo annual — cheapest dedicated platform, 3 users, 10-step framework
