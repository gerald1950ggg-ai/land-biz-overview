Zillow Manual STR Lookup

Quick steps to pull Active & Sold counts  ·  ~5 min for 3 counties
1
Go to Zillow
Open zillow.com
2
Search the county
Type in the search bar: Park County, CO (or Smith County, TX / White County, TN) → hit Enter
3
Set the filter to LAND
Click “Home Type” → uncheck everything → check “Lots/Land” only → click Apply
4
Set acreage to 1–5 acres
Click “More” → scroll to “Lot Size” → Min: 1 acre   Max: 5 acres → click Apply
5
Count the ACTIVE listings
Toggle at top should say “For Sale” → look at the result count at the top of the page (e.g., “218 results”) → write that down as Active
6
Switch to SOLD
Click the top toggle “For Sale” → switch to “Sold”
7
Set sold timeframe to 12 months
Click “Sold in last…” → select “12 months”
8
Count the SOLD listings
Look at the result count at the top of the page → write that down as Sold
Step 9 — Send Gerald the numbers
Just text back like this:
Park CO:    Active = ___    Sold = ___
Smith TX:   Active = ___    Sold = ___
White TN:   Active = ___    Sold = ___
Gerald calculates STR%, fills the scorecards, reprints.
STR % = Sold ÷ Active × 100   ·   Ideal: 20–130%   ·   Min Sold = 50 (skip if lower)   ·   Prepared by Gerald 🍊
