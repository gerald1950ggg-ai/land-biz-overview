List Source Comparison

Land Business — Marcus + Chris | Prepared May 22, 2026

Background

Investment Dominator (ID) is the CRM platform included in Jack Bosch's Land Profit Generator program. It charges $200+/month and includes a "Data Service" add-on for pulling land owner lists. That Data Service is powered by DataTree, owned by First American Data & Analytics — one of the largest property data companies in the US (150M+ properties, 100% nationwide coverage).

The question: Can we get the same data cheaper by going direct to the source, or through an alternative platform?

What Each Option Is Best For

Investment Dominator — Best if you want the full turnkey system Jack Bosch designed: CRM, deal flows, mail merge, websites, templates, coaching integration. You pay a premium for the convenience of everything being in one platform. Makes sense for someone who wants to follow the LPG system exactly as prescribed.

ListSource — Best for one-off list pulls with no commitment. Chris knows it. Good for testing a county before committing to a subscription anywhere. But no tools beyond the raw list — you handle everything else.

PropStream — Best bang for buck. $99/month gets you 25,000 exports (25x our first campaign), free skip tracing, comp tools, and marketing integration. The data is comprehensive and works in non-disclosure states. We'd use Gerald for the CRM/deal flow side that PropStream doesn't include.

DataTree Direct — Potentially the best if per-record pricing is competitive, because it's the same data ID uses without the markup. But pricing is opaque — need the free trial to evaluate. Best suited for someone who wants raw data access and will build their own tools on top (which is our approach with Gerald).

Recommendation

Short term: Start with PropStream Essentials ($99/month). Get the 7-day free trial first. Pull our first 1,000 leads for the initial campaign. Use the skip tracing and comp tools that come included. Gerald handles CRM, deal flow, and mail merge.

In parallel: Sign up for the DataTree free trial. Compare their per-record pricing to what we're getting from PropStream. If DataTree is cheaper and we can API-integrate with Gerald, we may switch for the long term.

Skip ID for now. At $294/month for the same underlying data, ID only makes sense if you want their full CRM — and Gerald replaces that.
