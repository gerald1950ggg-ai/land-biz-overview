## Critical Insight: Algo's Failure Mode

The dynamic heat guardrail works perfectly when:
✅ Market in strong uptrend
✅ Breakout success rate is high (37-41%)
✅ Winners keep winning, heat from winners frees up room for new entries

The algo FAILS when:
❌ Sector rotation is violent (growth→value→growth)
❌ Breakout success rate collapses (21% vs 37%)
❌ Meme stocks + IPOs + crypto distort price action
❌ "Hot sectors" mean-revert instead of compound

## Key Decisions Made

1. **Dynamic heat guardrail is GOOD** — no hard MAX_POSITIONS cap allows natural scaling
2. **TOP_N=20 is good** — increased from 10, lets through more setups
3. **But algo needs adaptive aggression** — must detect when breakout quality is poor and reduce heat dynamically

## Solution Path Forward

**Adaptive Aggression Filter**:
- Track rolling win rate on last 10 breakouts
- If WR < 30% for 2 days → reduce MAX_HEAT to 6%, reduce position sizing by 50%
- If WR > 40% for 3 days → increase MAX_HEAT to 12%, normal sizing
- This lets algo self-tune based on current market regime

This would have protected in Feb 2021 (detected 1 win in 12 trades, auto-reduced) and let loose in Jan 2021 (detected 5 wins in 12, full aggression).
