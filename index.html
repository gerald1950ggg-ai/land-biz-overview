🔄Deal Pipeline — 30-Day Cycle

Based on Jack Bosch's Land Profit method. Color = who owns the step.

1

Select Target Counties AI

Analyze population growth, land prices, sell-through rates, absentee owner data. Rank counties by opportunity score. Days 1

2

Pull & Filter Vacant Land Lists AI

Pull owner data via PropStream/ATTOM API. Filter: $10-300K value, 1-100 acres, absentee owners, no govt/corp. Days 2-3

3

Send AI-Personalized Direct Mail AI

Generate unique letters per owner via Lob.com/SendMailr. Personalized to parcel, county, owner situation. Not templates. Days 4-5

4

Process Inbound Responses AI

Voicemail transcription → AI lead scoring → comp research auto-attached → warm leads routed to Chris. Days 7-10

5

Chris Calls Warm Sellers CHRIS

Pre-qualified leads only. Comp data already attached. Friendly chat, build rapport, request to send written offer. Days 8-14

6

Make Written Offers CHRIS AI COMPS

AI generates offer range (5-35% of market). Chris chooses price, sends written offer. Offer tracking automated. Days 12-14

7

Follow Up on Non-Responses AI

Automated multi-touch follow-up sequences. Different messaging each touch. Re-route any new warm leads to Chris. Day 15+

8

Negotiate & Close CHRIS

Renegotiate if needed. Open escrow. Title company coordination. Sign contracts. Days 16-24

9

List Property for Sale AI

Auto-generate listings: LandWatch, Zillow, Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist. Photo descriptions. SEO optimized. Days 17-18

10

Sell & Collect CHRIS AI BUYERS

AI buyer matching + drip campaigns bring buyers. Chris negotiates final sale. Title company closes. Check deposited. Day 30 🎯

↻ Repeat every 6 weeks New mailer every cycle keeps the pipeline full. Multiple campaigns can overlap.
